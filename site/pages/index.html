{{icon:help:16}}Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. {{app}} is open source under the MIT License, with no paid tier, account or tracking.

Why is there no “Are you sure?” for every file?

Because cleaning a developer Mac means deleting hundreds of items, and a confirmation per item gets clicked through without reading. {{app}} explains each item instead, ticks only Safe ones for you, and can never delete protected folders. Prefer a safety net? Turn on Move to Trash.

Why does macOS block it the first time I open it?

The app is not notarized by Apple, which needs a paid developer membership. Open it once through System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. See the steps.
