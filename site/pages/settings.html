General

SettingWhat it doesDefault

Scanning

SettingWhat it doesDefault
ScanThe whole disk, or only your home folder. The whole disk also finds system caches and leftovers outside your home.Whole disk
Large files fromThe size from which a file appears in Large Files. The list updates as you change it.500 MB
Old downloads afterHow long a file in Downloads must be unchanged before it is suggested.90 days
Compare duplicates fromThe smallest file Duplicates compares.1 MB
Never scan these foldersFolders skipped completely: nothing in them is measured, suggested or deletable.None

Scope, the downloads age and excluded folders take effect at the next scan.
