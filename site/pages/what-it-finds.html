title: What Reclaim finds: every cache, dependency and leftover it suggests nav: What it finds icon: search description: Every folder Reclaim suggests deleting on a Mac: .next, node_modules, vendor, DerivedData, Gradle, npm and pip caches, Docker and system junk. lead: Every suggestion comes from one of these rules. A rule looks only at a folder's name, where it is and the files beside it; once a folder matches, nothing inside it is listed again. ---

~ means your home folder.

{{icon:info}}

Packages that tools installed for themselves are skipped: anything in a hidden folder in your home (such as ~/.vscode/extensions or ~/.composer), in ~/Library, or in system and Homebrew locations. Deleting those would break the tool, and there is no project to reinstall into.

App Leftovers

Folders in ~/Library/Containers and ~/Library/Application Support named after an app's identifier (such as com.docker.docker) when no installed app has that identifier. Apple's own folders are never included, and an extension's folder counts as its app's. Personal

If you reinstall the app later, it starts without its old settings and data.

System Junk

Most of these belong to root, so deleting them needs the helper. Items macOS protects are skipped even then.

WhatLocationLevel
System cacheeach folder in /Library/CachesSafe
System logseach item in /Library/Logs and /private/var/logSafe

Safe items that need the helper are only ticked for you once it is installed.

Never suggested or deleted

  • Anything inside an app, framework, photo or music library, or other bundle is never suggested on its own.
  • Folders you list under Never scan these folders.
  • Your Music, TV and Photos libraries, which macOS protects with permissions of their own.
  • Protected folders, which cannot be deleted as a whole from anywhere in the app. See the FAQ for the list.
