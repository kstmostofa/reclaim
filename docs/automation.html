ReclaimFeaturesMenu bar and schedules

Menu bar disk monitor, scheduled scans and auto-clean

Keep an eye on free space from the menu bar, and let Reclaim scan on a schedule so the disk never fills up unnoticed.

The menu bar

  • Free space and a small storage bar, with what the last scan found.
  • Scan Now starts a scan without opening the window.
  • Clean Safe Items deletes every Safe suggestion Reclaim can delete, then shows a notification with the space freed.
  • Open Reclaim, Settings and Quit.

Hide it with Show Reclaim in the menu bar. Scheduled scans and alerts keep running while Reclaim is open, even with its window closed.

Scheduled scans

Choose Every day or Every week in Automation › Scan automatically. A scan runs when one is due, including right after your Mac wakes, and by default only sends a notification such as “12.4 GB can be reclaimed, 3.1 GB of it Safe.”

Auto-clean

Off by default, and the only time Reclaim deletes something nobody ticked at that moment. When Delete Safe items untouched for a day is on, each scheduled scan deletes:

  • only Safe items, never Rebuild or Personal ones;
  • only items you can delete without the helper;
  • only items where nothing inside changed for 24 hours, so a project you are working in keeps its caches.

It follows your Trash setting and is recorded in the history, and a notification tells you what was freed.

Low-space alerts

Reclaim checks free space every few minutes and notifies you once when it drops below your threshold, 10 GB by default, and again only after it has recovered and dropped again.

Open at login

Turn on Open at login in Automation so the menu bar and schedules are always running. If macOS asks you to approve it, Reclaim shows a button to Login Items in System Settings.

Notifications

The first time you turn on a schedule or low-space alerts, macOS asks whether Reclaim may send notifications. You can change this later in System Settings › Notifications.
